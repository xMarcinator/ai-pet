# fn-2-layered-sprite-and-vector-pets.16 Recolourable vector packs: a named palette and recolour files

## Description
Let a vector pack be recoloured without editing its drawing (asked by the owner on 2026-11-07, after the vector Hood (green) shipped).

**Size:** S–M
**Files:** `rust/crates/aipet-sprite/src/{manifest.rs,vector.rs,pack.rs,load.rs}`, `avatars/reference/vector_pack.py`, `avatars/reference/pip_gen.py`, the vector packs, `rust/crates/aipet-ui/src/settings/avatars.rs` (later, optional)

### Approach
- A pack names its colours in a `palette` (`{"robe": "#0F4422", "trim": "#2BD46A", "eyes": "#5CFF9C", …}`), and a shape's `fill` or `stroke` may be `$name` instead of a hex colour. An unknown name skips the pack with a reason; a palette entry is a colour like any other (no URLs).
- The pack's `accent` and `glow` may also name palette colours.
- Recolour by a small file in the avatars folder: `{"base": "<pack name>", "name": "Hood (red)", "palette": {"robe": "#4A1010", …}}`. It loads as its own pet (all-or-nothing, same limits), overriding only the colours it names; unknown names are a reason line.
- The generators emit the palette and `$name` references for the colours a person would want to change (body, trim, eyes and glow), and keep fixed colours (effects, the magnifier) as they are.
- The painted bake is unaffected (it paints whatever colours the pack has); recolouring a painted or pixel pack is out of scope.
## Acceptance
- [ ] A vector pack with a `palette` and `$name` colours loads and draws as before; an unknown `$name` or bad palette colour skips it with a reason (tests)
- [ ] A recolour file in the avatars folder shows up as its own pet with the new colours; a bad base or unknown colour name is a skipped line in Settings (tests)
- [ ] The built-in vector Hood (green), Hamster, Sprout and Pip name their main colours, regenerated by the generators (the regeneration test still passes)
- [ ] `--check-pack` accepts a recolour file's folder or reports why not
