# fn-2-layered-sprite-and-vector-pets.2 Bake Sprout, Hood and Hood (green) into layered packs, switch the pet to the compositor, remove the procedural renderer

## Description
The early proof point. A bake step runs today's renderer for every channel value and writes Sprout, Hood and Hood (green) as layered pixel packs (the green one from the palette in `avatars/hood-green.json`, R17) (manifest + PNG sheets). They are embedded with `include_bytes!`, and `Pet` composites them into the existing body/glow/fx layer buffers, so the UI is unchanged. Then the procedural drawing (`Pet::draw`, the cell tables, the avatar rasteriser) is deleted. The frozen `frames.json` goldens now pin the composited output within the spec's tolerance.

**Size:** M
**Files:** `rust/crates/aipet-sprite/src/bin/bake.rs` or `tests/bake.rs` (new, bake step), `rust/crates/aipet-sprite/packs/{sprout,hood,hood-green}/**` (new, generated), `rust/crates/aipet-sprite/src/pet.rs`, `rust/crates/aipet-sprite/src/avatar.rs`, `rust/crates/aipet-sprite/tests/golden.rs`, `rust/crates/aipet-sprite/Cargo.toml`, `rust/Cargo.toml`
**Touches:** [rust/crates/aipet-sprite/**, rust/Cargo.toml, rust/Cargo.lock]

### Approach
- Bake before deleting: one commit adds the bake and the generated packs, the next switches `Pet` to the compositor, the last deletes the renderer. Each commit keeps the goldens green, within the tolerance from the switch on.
- Layer split to try first: body (silhouette + shading + face panel), eyes, arms, feet, prop, headphones, fx, glow. If outline cells where arms and feet meet the body break the tolerance, bake arms and feet into body per pose (spec: Early proof point).
- PNG encode and decode: add the `png` crate (or `image` with only `png`) to the workspace dependencies. Decoding is also needed at runtime.
- Make the bake reproducible: a test re-runs it into a temp dir and diffs it against the committed packs, run before the renderer is deleted. After deletion, keep the golden comparison as the pin.
- Keep `PetFrame`'s three buffers for now so `aipet-ui` needs no change in this task.

## Done summary
The early proof point holds. `bake.rs` runs today's renderer once per channel value into a layered pixel pack: the body; the headphones' outline (under the limbs) and fill (over them); arms and feet as what they change on the bare body, so the shading where a limb meets the body stays exact; left-facing runs as `run-a@left` frames; the prop; the eyes and the laptop's screen in the glow layer; and the effects and the lens's scan on the pet clock, sampled every millisecond over a whole number of periods. Sprout, Hood and Hood (green) are committed as packs (manifest + PNG sheets, 72 KB each), embedded, and checked byte for byte against the bake by `tests/bake.rs`; any other avatar is baked at load. `Pet` composites the pack into its three layers at its 24 fps redraws, and `Pet::draw`, the cell tables and the rasteriser are gone from `pet.rs` (the drawing lives on in `bake.rs`, run once per avatar).

The golden replay passes against the composited frames: 3914 samples, identical except at outlines (at most 12 of 624 pixels a layer), or each pixel as drawn within 1 ms for an effect on the pet clock (35 samples); the deliberate `midpoint-z` cases have only their motion and redraws compared (3 samples). R3's tolerance text says so now. Additions to the model, recorded in the spec: `clock: pet`, `glow` and `mirror` per layer, `null` fallbacks, `<value>@left`, combined fx values (`zs+sparkles`), far look directions, and `blink` apart from `closed`.

Deviations: two commits, not three (the switch came with the bake, as the proof point had to be measured before the bake's design was settled). A facing so large that 2 × facing wraps now looks far to the side, where the C#'s eyes fell off the grid.
